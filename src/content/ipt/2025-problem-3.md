# Problem 3: Cosmic Ray Origin

## Statement

Investigate the origin and acceleration mechanisms of ultra-high-energy cosmic rays (UHECRs). Analyze composition measurements and arrival direction anisotropies.

## Requirements

- Acceleration mechanism models for UHECRs
- Propagation effects through cosmic microwave background
- Correlation with potential astrophysical sources

## Background

The origin of cosmic rays above 10^18 eV remains unknown. Composition and anisotropy data from Auger and Telescope Array provide crucial constraints.
