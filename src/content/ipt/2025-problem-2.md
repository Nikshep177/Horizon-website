# Problem 2: Neutrino Astronomy

## Statement

Explore multi-messenger astronomy with high-energy neutrinos. Correlate neutrino events with electromagnetic and gravitational wave observations.

## Requirements

- Neutrino telescope angular resolution requirements
- Real-time alert system design for follow-up observations
- Source population modeling for astrophysical neutrinos

## Background

IceCube has detected high-energy astrophysical neutrinos. Identifying their sources requires coordinated multi-messenger campaigns.
