# Problem 1: Exoplanet Atmosphere Characterization

## Statement

Develop methods for characterizing atmospheres of temperate exoplanets using transmission and emission spectroscopy. Focus on biosignature gas detection.

## Requirements

- Signal-to-noise requirements for key biosignature gases
- Observing strategy for JWST and future telescopes
- False positive mitigation strategies

## Background

Characterizing exoplanet atmospheres is the next frontier in the search for life beyond Earth. Temperate planets in habitable zones are prime targets.
