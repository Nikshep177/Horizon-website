# Problem 4: Gravitational Wave Detection Methods

## Statement

Analyze novel methods for detecting high-frequency gravitational waves beyond the LIGO/Virgo frequency band. Consider resonant mass detectors, atom interferometry, and other emerging technologies.

## Requirements

- Sensitivity calculations for proposed detector designs
- Noise budget analysis for each technology
- Astrophysical source predictions for high-frequency band

## Background

Current gravitational wave detectors operate in the 10 Hz - 10 kHz range. High-frequency waves (kHz - MHz) could reveal new physics and exotic compact objects.
