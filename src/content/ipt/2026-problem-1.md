# Problem 1: Thermal Radiation in Space

## Statement

Investigate the thermal radiation properties of spacecraft materials in vacuum conditions. Consider the effects of material composition, surface roughness, and temperature gradients on radiative heat transfer.

## Requirements

- Theoretical analysis of Stefan-Boltzmann law applications
- Experimental design for vacuum chamber testing
- Data analysis framework for thermal imaging results

## Background

Spacecraft thermal management is critical for mission success. Understanding how materials radiate heat in the vacuum of space enables better thermal control system design.
