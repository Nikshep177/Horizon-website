# Problem 3: Quantum Entanglement in Astrophysics

## Statement

Explore potential applications of quantum entanglement in astronomical observations. Investigate whether entangled photon pairs can enhance telescope resolution or sensitivity.

## Requirements

- Theoretical framework for quantum-enhanced interferometry
- Feasibility analysis for space-based quantum telescopes
- Noise and decoherence considerations in space environment

## Background

Quantum technologies promise revolutionary improvements in measurement precision. Applying these to astronomy could enable direct imaging of exoplanets and other breakthrough observations.
