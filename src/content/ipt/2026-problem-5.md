# Problem 5: Dark Matter Direct Detection

## Statement

Design an experiment for direct detection of low-mass dark matter particles (sub-GeV range). Consider novel target materials, readout technologies, and background rejection strategies.

## Requirements

- Target material selection criteria and properties
- Signal-to-noise ratio calculations for proposed design
- Background discrimination techniques

## Background

Traditional WIMP searches focus on GeV-TeV masses. Sub-GeV dark matter requires fundamentally different detection approaches using electron recoils, phonons, or other low-energy signals.
