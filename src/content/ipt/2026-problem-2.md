# Problem 2: Magnetic Field Interactions

## Statement

Study the interaction between planetary magnetic fields and solar wind particles. Analyze the formation of magnetospheres and their protective effects on planetary atmospheres.

## Requirements

- Magnetohydrodynamic simulation approach
- Analysis of magnetic reconnection events
- Comparison with observational data from space missions

## Background

Planetary magnetic fields shield atmospheres from solar wind erosion. Understanding these interactions is crucial for planetary science and space weather prediction.
